# GEMINI.md Configuration

## Application Scope
ARIS (Antenatal Records & Information System for Ghana Health Service)

## Capabilities & Integrations
- **Server-side Gemini API Integration** for AI medical clinical summaries and risk stratification.
- **Khaya AI Integration** for West African local languages (Dagbani, Twi, Ewe, Fante, Ga):
  - Voice dictation for ANC medical note logging.
  - Audio voice feedback and TTS playback for translated instructions.
  - Community health SMS and audio campaign generation.
- **Full-stack Express + Vite architecture** listening on port 3000.
